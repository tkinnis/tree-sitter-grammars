# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise the
code and not the person writing it.

## What is expected

- Take the most reasonable reading of what somebody wrote.
- Say what you mean plainly, including when you disagree.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change that is good work,
  and that a contributor may decline to do more than they offered.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning remarks about who somebody is,
unwanted attention after being asked to stop, and deliberately
derailing a discussion. Publishing somebody's private information.
Sustained disruption.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space
this project uses, and to conduct elsewhere that reasonably affects
someone's safety in these ones.

## Enforcement

Report a concern through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on
this repository, which reaches the maintainer privately, or by opening
an issue if it is something that can be discussed in the open.

This is a one-maintainer project, so enforcement is one person's
judgement: a warning, a removed comment, or a block, in that order and
skipping steps where the conduct warrants it. A report will be answered
and will not be shared beyond what answering it requires.
